Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1.Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and password. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and notes. We do not collect payment card details or connect to your bank accounts.

2.How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to record and display your expenses, and to improve the service. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3.Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and retained for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, and we will remove it within a reasonable period.

4.Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your account or expense data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5.Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact the Spendly team at support@spendly.example.
